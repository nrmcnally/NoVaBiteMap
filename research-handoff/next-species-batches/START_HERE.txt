BiteMap remaining-species research starter pack
================================================

For the first new ChatGPT conversation, attach these three files from this folder:

1. BiteMap_complete_37_species_research_archive.zip
2. BiteMap_channel-catfish_deep_verified_research_archive.zip
3. COPY_PASTE_MASTER_PROMPT.txt

Then paste the contents of COPY_PASTE_MASTER_PROMPT.txt into the conversation.
It is already configured for BATCH 1.

For later batches:

1. Start a new conversation to avoid the context-length limit.
2. Attach the two baseline archives above.
3. Attach the ZIP produced by the immediately preceding batch.
4. Paste the master prompt again.
5. Change "ACTIVE BATCH = BATCH 1" to the next batch number.

The three completed species that should not be researched again are:

- largemouth-bass
- smallmouth-bass
- channel-catfish

Important validation note:

The channel-catfish archive is the structural and quality template, but its
validation report missed one bibliography problem. Source S13's original paper
lists Kenneth O. Allen AND James W. Avault Jr. Every new report must verify the
complete author byline against the original PDF title page.
